## Assumptions and limitations

- Each window starts with the same initial equity, an empty account and no pause state. Out-of-sample P&L is added, not compounded. Window resets can hide persistent risk halts.
- All runs close within their final hour, including fees, slippage and funding. This truncates long S1 trends. Market signals fill at the next bar open; S2-P limit entries require strict crossing before expiry. Ambiguous intrabar events prioritize the stop.
- Stop-query confirmation may lag by one hourly boundary and block new entries during that boundary (M2-03 P3).
- Instrument rules and risk tiers are pinned collection-time snapshots, not historical snapshots.
- Optional one-minute ambiguity revalidation was not performed. Hourly OHLC cannot reconstruct intrabar paths.
- INVALID_DATA and NO_CANDIDATE windows are flat and count toward the invalid-window limit. UNSET means pass criteria were omitted. PASS requires strictly positive configured excess P&L and drawdown strictly below the configured limit.
- This is one explicitly configured small grid; no tuning was performed using test results. Fees/slippage and all risk limits are recorded above.
